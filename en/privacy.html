Privacy Policy

Coupocke (hereinafter referred to as "the App") prioritizes your privacy and handles all information with the utmost care. This policy provides transparent information regarding our data practices in compliance with Apple's App Store guidelines and international privacy standards.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The App may collect and use information for the following purposes:

  • Location Data: Used by the Smart Radar feature to find nearby shops and send proximity notifications (including when running in the background).
  • Motion Data (Activity): Detects walking or driving states to optimize notification timing based on your travel speed and to reduce battery consumption.
  • Advertising Identifiers (IDFA, etc.): Used through Google AdMob to deliver optimized advertisements related to your interests.
  • Facebook SDK: Used for app event tracking, analytics, and service improvement.
  • In-App Data: Used for OCR analysis and management of coupon information (shop name, expiry date, images).
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Anonymously collects and analyzes app usage (screen views, feature usage, app launches, etc.) to improve our service. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

2. Proper Handling of Location Data

To enable notification features, we may request permission to access your location in the background. Location data is used **only within the App** for distance calculation and proximity detection. **We do not transmit, store on external servers, or sell your location data for tracking or any other purposes.**

3. Data Storage and Security

All coupon images and text data you register in the App are **stored exclusively on your personal device's secure storage (SwiftData/Keychain, etc.)**. The developer does not have access to your individual coupon data.

4. Data Management and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing individual coupons within the App. Additionally, deleting the App from your device will physically remove all associated data stored locally.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

This App uses "Google AdMob" and "Facebook SDK (Meta)," which may collect anonymized identifiers for advertising and analytics purposes. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy for more details.

This App also uses Google LLC's "Firebase Analytics" to collect statistical information about app usage and device identifiers (such as the app instance ID) for service improvement. This data is collected anonymously and does not identify individuals. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Use of AI Services

This App utilizes Google's "Gemini API" to analyze coupon text information. Only after you provide explicit consent within the App, text data extracted from coupon images is sent to Google's servers for analysis purposes only.

  • Transmitted data is used solely for the analysis process and is **not used for training AI models**.
  • For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Disclaimer

While we strive for accuracy, we are not responsible for delays or failures in notifications due to OCR analysis errors or communication issues. Always verify the actual coupon details yourself.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us via our Contact Form.
